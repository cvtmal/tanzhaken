TanzHaken

The attendance sheet, without the sheet.

A calm, mobile-first web app for independent dance teachers. The teacher opens a lesson, students scan one QR code, and a credit comes off their 10-lesson pass.

Foundations A · Login B · Today C · Courses D · New course E · Course F · Lesson G · QR display H · Student check-in
Direction

Paper, ink and one plum. A warm paper ground, near-white cards and a single deep accent that only ever marks what you can act on. Green, amber and red are reserved for attendance and pass balance, never decoration.

Decisions
  • One filled button per screen. Everything else is quiet.
  • The tick (Haken) is the brand and the confirmation.
  • A pass is ten dots, the paper punch card kept.
  • No tables on mobile. Rows open in place, one at a time.
Assumptions
  • English copy, Swiss conventions (24 h, Tue 10 Nov).
  • Credits belong to the student, not to one course.
  • The dashboard is labelled “Today”.
  • The brief stops midway through Screen H. The name list, success and low-pass states are my reading of it.
