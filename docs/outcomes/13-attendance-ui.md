# #13 attendance-ui — outcome (2026-11-11)

## Delivered
The teacher's lesson page and the student's attendance history, on top of #7's `AttendanceMarker` / `LessonRegister` and #6's `Balances`. `/courses/{id}/lessons/{lessonId}` shows the course name, the lesson's date and time in the course zone, a status badge (Scheduled / Completed / Cancelled), "n of m present" and the register: one row per student (last name order) with Not marked / Present / Absent, the time and source of the mark ("by you" / "QR"), the balance chip, and "Archived" / "Left the course" badges. Each markable row is a `<details>` with its own POST form: optional reason, Present / Absent, a cost line ("Present uses 1 lesson. Alex would then have 5 left …") and Clear. "Complete lesson" appears once a scheduled lesson has started; "Cancel this lesson" (a disclosure, no dialog) while no row is marked, otherwise the hint "Clear the marks to cancel this lesson". The student page (#9's slot) gets an "Attendance" section: every mark across the teacher's courses, newest first, each linking to its lesson. Works without JS. One controller, one read-model wrapper, one form, one Twig extension, three templates, two stylesheets, the `attendance` catalogue. No migration, no config change; `lessonShow()` is deleted from `src/Attendance/Controller/PlaceholderController.php` (`courseExport()` stays for #15).

## Interfaces
- **Routes** (`Controller\LessonController`, `ROLE_TEACHER`, every id `Requirement::UUID`): `lesson_show` GET `/courses/{id}/lessons/{lessonId}`; `lesson_mark` POST `/courses/{id}/lessons/{lessonId}/attendance/{studentId}` (buttons `present` / `absent` / `clear`, optional `reason`); `lesson_complete` POST `/courses/{id}/lessons/{lessonId}/complete`. Cancel posts to #10's `course_lesson_cancel` (its redirect and flashes). Foreign / unknown / malformed id, or a lesson of another course → 404 (also #10's `Courses\Exception\NotFound` from `LessonScheduler::complete()`); every POST → 303 to `lesson_show` (`#student-<rfc4122>` after a row action) with a `success` / `error` flash, also after `changedConcurrently`.

## Decisions
- **P1** One POST route per row with three buttons (one reason field shared by change and clear, no JS); replaces research's separate `lesson_clear`. Undo: split `clear` into its own route and form.
- **P2/P3** Cancel reuses #10's `CancelLessonType` → `course_lesson_cancel` (redirects to `course_show`), in a `<details>` without dialog (Reinstate on the course page undoes it). Undo: `_dialog` + `page_scripts = ['dialog']`.
- **P4** `LessonRegisterEntry` wraps the register row and its balance; the cost line is Balances ±1 (S2).
- **P5** `can_mark` = lesson not cancelled and enrollment active; `can_clear` = row has a mark. Left student: "Left the course", Clear only; archived student: badge, markable; cancelled lesson: notice, Clear only.
- **#7 D6 — lessons in the future may be marked.** `can_mark` does not depend on time (P5): a future lesson's register is markable, as the marker allows. Only Complete waits for the start (P6). Undo: add `startsAtUtc <= now` to `can_mark` in `LessonController::show()`.
- **P6** Complete when `scheduled` and started; Cancel when not cancelled and unmarked, else the hint (S9). Neither is offered on an archived course (the scheduler refuses both).
- **P7** Flashes `attendance.flash.*` with "Alex M."; errors from `translationKey()`; `InvalidCourse` from complete in domain `courses`. **P8** Tests reuse #10's `CoursePages` plus a `LessonPages` trait.

## Deviations
- Validator message uses ICU `{limit}`, not `{{ limit }}` (`+intl-icu` catalogue); `reason` has `attr.maxlength` 255 as #10's `CancelLessonType`.
- Template variables named by the implementation (`mark_forms`, `complete_form`, `cancel_form`, `cancel_blocked`); `present_count` counts a left student's present mark too, `roster_count` only active enrollments.
- The success flash's name is read from the register before the write (a left student's cleared row then vanishes); only a race leaves `{name}` empty. `handle()` also maps #10's `NotFound` → 404.
- Subtitle composed from `lesson.show.subtitle|with_location|with_timezone` (as #10); `<title>` = `lesson.show.title`. Empty register links "Add a student" to `course_show#enroll` (not on an archived course); ≥ 64rem a header row Student / Checked in / Lessons left (`aria-hidden`).
- Row `<summary>` has no `aria-label` (it would hide status and balance from screen readers); a visually hidden "Change attendance" instead. Status line "Present · 7:58 PM · QR"; reason field above the buttons, cost line below; current status's button `soft`.
- History rows: no date tile, full date with year in the meta line; reuses #9's `.students-section` classes. Extra test: an archived course offers neither Complete nor Cancel; refusals (cancelled lesson, left student) tested with crafted POSTs.
- **#2's `tests/Functional/Shared/LayoutTest.php` edited**: its lesson-page test used the deleted placeholder's fake ids (`/courses/x/lessons/y`, now 404 by the UUID requirements); it builds an owned course and lesson with factories and keeps its assertion (Courses nav current). `PlaceholderRoutesTest` lists the three routes as shipped.
- Step 6.4 browser fixes: desktop balance column 7rem → 11rem with a wrapping label; below 64rem the balance label (Low / Renewal needed / n outstanding) is visually hidden, chip only (F1).
- Design differences kept: no check-in panel / QR / "Open check-in" bar (#16 slot); no segmented present bar, text "n of m present"; no toast Undo; 12-hour times (en locale, D-042); balance as chip, not "6 left" / F3's plain "Left" column; status badge at the header's right, beyond the 48rem register on wide screens (F3 puts the QR panel there); mark buttons behind the row disclosure, not inline on hover; breadcrumbs "Courses › course › Tue 10 Nov", not F3's "Today › …".
- Complete was checked by hand on a lesson set back to `scheduled` in the dev DB (the seed has no started scheduled lesson); re-seeded afterwards.
